Software Development Agreement

This agreement is between RS Dev (the Provider), represented by Hafiz Subhan Sabir, and [Client legal name] (the Client).

1. The work

RS Dev will carry out the work described in the attached proposal and quotation. Hafiz Subhan will act as Project Manager and Full Stack Engineer for the delivery. If the scope needs to change, we will write it down together. Price or timing may change with it.

2. What the client will do

  • Share access, content, and feedback without long delays
  • Name one person who can approve milestones
  • Pay invoices on the agreed schedule

3. Fees

Fees follow the quotation. If payment is late, we may pause work after a written notice. Any tax that applies is the client’s duty unless we agree otherwise in writing.

4. Who owns the work

When a deliverable is paid in full, the client owns the custom work made for that deliverable. RS Dev keeps its own tools, templates, and general methods. Third party software stays under its own licenses.

5. Keeping things private

Both sides will keep private information private and use it only for this project. This duty lasts three years after the work ends, or longer for secrets that stay secret.

6. Quality and acceptance

We will do the work with care. After each milestone, the client has __ working days to accept it or to report real problems in writing. If we hear nothing, we treat it as accepted.

7. Limits of responsibility

Except for fraud, serious misconduct, or a clear break of privacy rules, each side’s total responsibility under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the three months before the claim. Neither side is responsible for indirect losses.

8. Ending the agreement

Either side may end the agreement if the other breaks it and does not fix the break within 14 days of notice. The client may also end for convenience with 14 days’ notice. In that case, RS Dev bills for finished work and costs that cannot be cancelled. Any non refundable deposit stays as written in the quotation.

9. Working relationship

RS Dev works as an independent contractor, not as an employee or partner of the client.

10. Law

This agreement follows the laws of [Pakistan / agreed place]. If we disagree, we try to settle first, then use the courts of [City].

11. Whole agreement

This document plus the proposal and quotation are the full agreement for this work. They replace earlier talks on the same subject.
