## 5. Voice & pitching style

- **Tone:** Direct, confident, warm — short sentences, no jargon stacks.
- **Proof:** Prefer specifics (“JWT + RBAC”, “payment flows”, “Core Web Vitals”) over superlatives (“#1”, “world-class”).
- **CTA ladder:** Soft → strong: “See selected work” → “Tell us the constraint” → “Book a 20-minute scope call.”
- **What to avoid:** Mirrored phrases from large vendor sites; fake statistics; logos you do not have rights to display.

## 9. What we build — service cards (original descriptions)

### Websites & web applications

Marketing sites, landing systems, customer portals, and internal web apps — responsive, fast, and structured so SEO and analytics can be implemented properly.

### Custom software

Bespoke tools and business logic when off-the-shelf products do not fit: workflows, admin panels, integrations, and long-lived codebases with clear handoff.

### CRM solutions

Selection guidance, implementation, customization, pipelines, reporting, and integrations so sales and support data stay consistent with your product and campaigns.

### SEO & ranking

Technical SEO (structure, performance, indexation), keyword and page intent alignment, and practical recommendations — aligned with content you can actually maintain.

### AI agents & AI bots

Conversational or task-oriented assistants grounded in your FAQs, policies, or APIs — with boundaries, logging, and fallbacks suitable for real customers or staff.

### AI workflow automation

Connect AI steps to triggers and systems you already use (email, CRM, ticketing, spreadsheets): reduce copy-paste, speed approvals, and keep humans in the loop where it matters.

### Digital marketing

Channel strategy, campaign structure, tracking setup, and messaging that matches your product story — as execution support or consultancy alongside your team.

### Consultancy

Roadmaps, vendor/tool choices, architecture and security basics, AI use-case feasibility, and “what to build first” — standalone or bundled with delivery.

### API design, auth & integrations (often bundled)

REST and third-party integrations, authentication patterns, and contracts between web apps, CRM, and automation layers.

### Launch & operations readiness

Security basics, monitoring hooks, documentation, and runbooks so launches are not a single-day gamble.

## 10. How delivery works (4 phases — rename if you like)

1. **Align** — Goals, users, constraints, success signals. Short written brief.
2. **Shape** — Architecture sketch, UI direction, milestone plan with explicit tradeoffs.
3. **Build** — Incremental demos, PR-sized chunks, staging you can click through.
4. **Handoff** — README, env template, runbooks, and optional knowledge transfer call.
